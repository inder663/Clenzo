Privacy Policy

Clenzo is built to clean your gallery and contacts privately. Your media stays on your iPhone unless you choose otherwise.

1. Overview

Clenzo (“we”, “us”) is an iOS app by DevHook. This policy explains what the app accesses on your device and what we do not collect.

In short: analysis and cleanup run on your iPhone. We do not operate a Clenzo account system, and we do not upload your photo library or contacts to our servers.

2. Data we access

With your permission, Clenzo may access:

  • Photos library — to scan, categorize, preview, delete, favorite, album, share, and move items into or out of Vault.
  • Contacts — to find duplicate or incomplete contacts and let you merge or delete them.
  • Face ID / Touch ID — only to unlock Vault if you enable biometrics (optional; you can use a PIN instead).
  • Local notifications — if you turn on reminders or storage tips in Settings.

Permission prompts appear in the system UI. You can change access anytime in iOS Settings → Clenzo.

3. How we use data

  • Detect duplicates, screenshots, documents, people photos, blurry shots, similar bursts, and other cleanup categories using on-device frameworks (PhotoKit, Vision, and related Apple APIs).
  • Show storage estimates and suggestions so you can reclaim space.
  • Help clean contacts that share the same phone, email, or name, or that are incomplete.
  • Keep private media in Vault after you import it, protected by your PIN / biometrics.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use your library for advertising.

4. Storage & Vault

Scan results and preferences are stored locally on your device (for example in app storage / SwiftData / Keychain for the Vault PIN hash).

Vault copies selected photos and videos into Clenzo’s private app container. Those files are excluded from iCloud backup where we can configure that, and protected with iOS file protection. Deleting the app removes Vault contents unless you restored them to Photos first.

5. Sharing

Clenzo does not send your photos or contacts to DevHook servers as part of normal use.

If you use Share, Mail, or other system sheets, content is handled by those apps and their privacy policies. Links you open (for example this policy) may be loaded by Safari or another browser.

If we add optional analytics or cloud features later, we will update this policy and ask for consent where required.

6. Your choices

  • Deny or revoke Photos / Contacts / Face ID / notification permission in iOS Settings.
  • Delete items from Vault or move them back to Photos.
  • Delete the app to remove local Clenzo data from the device.
  • Contact us to ask privacy questions (see below).

7. Children

Clenzo is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region). Do not use the app if you are under that age.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Effective” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
